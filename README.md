# Carbon Constellation: The Living Internet Carbon & Physical Infrastructure Engine

> *"The internet feels weightless. It isn't."*

Carbon Constellation is an interactive, spatial 3D visualization and simulation engine that reveals the tangible physical reality, electrical energy consumption, water cooling impact, and carbon footprint of global internet traffic.

---

## 🌌 Overview

Every single click, search, stream, and page load transfers megabytes across a physical global machine: local Wi-Fi radios, terrestrial optical backbones, subsea ocean-floor cables, and hyperscale data centers. 

Carbon Constellation transforms abstract sustainability metrics into an intuitive, tactile experience:
- **3D Celestial Constellation**: Global websites mapped as celestial bodies whose orbital radius, luminosity, and gravitational envelope reflect real-world payload weight and grid carbon intensity.
- **7-Stage Living Infrastructure Walkthrough**: Guided cinematic tour walking through the physics of single clicks, submarine fiber cables, server hall evaporative cooling, and planetary concurrency up to 5.4 Billion users.
- **Multi-Perspective Views**:
  - **3D Constellation View**: Spatial WebGL orbital mechanics showing verified green PPAs vs. fossil-heavy grid hosting.
  - **Physical Infrastructure Matrix**: Regional data center carbon intensities, cooling tower water consumption, and Power Usage Effectiveness (PUE).
  - **Packet Journey Transit Simulator**: Step-by-step photon transmission through optical repeaters and edge caching.
- **Scenario Simulator ("What-If" Engine)**: Live modeling of 100% 24/7 carbon-free grids, edge caching, and asset compression.

---

## ⚡ Features

- **Real-Time Physics & WebGL Rendering**: Built with Three.js for 60 FPS spatial interactions, particle pulses, pinch-to-zoom, and touch rotation.
- **Sustainable Web Design Model (SWDM v4 & GHG Protocol)**: Accurate carbon modeling combining:
  - Data transfer energy: $0.004 \text{ kWh/MB}$
  - Hosting PUE & regional grid emission factors ($g\text{CO}_2e/\text{kWh}$)
  - Chiller evaporation water footprint ($1.8 \text{ L/kWh}$)
- **Interactive Concurrency Scaling**: Seamlessly scale from a single visit ($1\times$) to $10\times$, $1\text{K}$, $1\text{M}$ sessions, $50\text{M}$ spikes, up to $5.4\text{B}$ global internet users.
- **Side-by-Side Comparison Engine**: Compare platforms directly (e.g. YouTube vs. Wikipedia vs. Google).
- **AI-Powered Climate Diagnostics**: Context-aware recommendations and emissions breakdown powered by Gemini.
- **100% Mobile & Responsive Design**: Full touch gesture support, pinch-to-zoom, and responsive HUD overlays.
